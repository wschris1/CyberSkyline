# This Way Pleaae!

Two intercepted courier messages describe a plan involving a Fortune 10 company's US office:

> Message 1: Let's set up at the Museum of the Arts Gym. Then, we can move to the Estoril stop and the carpenter stop.
>
> Message 2: Sure, and then after infiltrating, let's meet at River Bend at 8 PM. I'll put a maintenance sign outside, the package is in the corner.

1. What program contains the setup locations referenced by the messages?
2. In what city is the office?
3. In what year was the office completed?
4. What location type and alphanumeric code correspond to “River Bend”? Submit both the location type and its code.
