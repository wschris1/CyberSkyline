# Friendly Archive Upload

An intern at Friendly and Associates tried to upload a personal archive through a company tool, but the transfer failed. IT recovered the encoded upload and wants to determine what data was being sent.

The password policy required at least eight characters, including a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter, a digit, and a symbol. The prompt hints that the password pattern dates from before the 2010s.

1. What is the ZIP archive password?
2. What is the password for the encrypted file inside the ZIP?
3. What is the flag?

The encoded upload and any recovered files are not stored here.
