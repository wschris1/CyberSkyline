## Prompt

NOTE: Challenge Instance will not be accessible once challenge is completed.

Liber8tion ransomware hit a company file share and encrypted everything on it, including the disaster-recovery pack. The offsite backups can't be restored without the recovery reference, and the only copy of it was in that pack. Helpdesk pulled a folder of the encrypted files off the share, but the malware's key was never recovered. The only other thing that survived is a short fragment of an old document found on a laptop, and it may be out of date. Can you recover the correct recovery reference \(the flag\) from the encrypted files?

## Question 1

Q1 - 70 points



What is the correct recovery reference? Note that the correct reference does not contain any zeroes. Answer format: ` [FLAG REMOVED] `
