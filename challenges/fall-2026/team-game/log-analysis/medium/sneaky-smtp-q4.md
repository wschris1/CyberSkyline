## Prompt

Meridian Fabrication Co. is a mid-size industrial design firm. Their SOC received an automated alert: a workstation in the Engineering VLAN \(` 10.12.4.0/24 `\) submitted an unusually large number of outbound emails through the internal mail relay over a 48-hour window. We have provided the Zeek logs for the relay machine scoped to SMTP traffic. We need you to figure out what happened, what was taken, who did it, and where it went.

## Question 4

Q4 - 10 points
