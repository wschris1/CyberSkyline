## Prompt

K-Psych Digital Media released the MMORPG Empires of Il'thon \(EI\) last year. It's been performing well though critics note a lack of item and class diversity. On September 30th, a change to the in-game NPC run trade board was pushed into production by an ex-employee and member of Liber8tion that introduced an infinite money glitch. It is believed this was meant to set up a gold-farming system for selling gold to other players for cash.

Your job is to identify potential transaction patterns, using the proprietary log format, that could indicate users that were manipulating the system to generate gold and then identify those users.

## Question 8

Q8 - 5 points

What was the solo glitch farmer's net change in gold by the end of the day?
