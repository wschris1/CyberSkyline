## Prompt

We suspect that Liber8tion has been using radio for obfuscated communications, and in response we have started monitoring the airwaves near their hideouts. The attached transmission was recently picked up by our operatives, but when we play it back, we just hear garbled tones that don't sound very useful. However, we believe that a spectrogram could help reveal what kind of signal it is and how to decode it further. See what you can find\!
