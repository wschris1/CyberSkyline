# Choclympic Peaks Product Leak

A chocolate shop's unreleased seasonal bar appeared on a fan forum before its announcement. Analyze the supplied `access.log` for the attacker who visited on three days, their exact User-Agent, the traversal-prone site path, their first traversal test, the leaked product slug, the successful data-file request, and its date.

The web access log is a supplied challenge artifact and is not stored in this repository.
