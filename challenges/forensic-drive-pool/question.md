# Forensic Drive Pool

A supplied drive image appears to hold two flags. Investigate the filesystem and its pool metadata.

1. What filesystem is in use?
2. What is the pool name?
3. What is the pool identifier?
4. What is the disk size in shorthand?
5. When was the pool last scrubbed, rounded to the nearest minute in UTC?
6. What is the full name of the snapshot protected from deletion?
7. What is the first flag?
8. What is the second flag?

The original drive image was supplied as a compressed file and is not stored here.
