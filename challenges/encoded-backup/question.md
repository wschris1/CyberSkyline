# Encoded Backup

A deleted Slack message contained a long block of text that appears to encode a personal backup. Determine what is inside the archive.

Both passwords are at least eight characters long and each contains a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter, a digit, and a symbol.

1. What is the password to the archive?
2. What is the password to the encrypted file inside the archive?
3. What is the flag? Its format is `SKY-ABCD-####`.

The encoded message and its contents are challenge evidence and are not stored in this repository.
