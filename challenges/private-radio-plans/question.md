# Private Radio Plans

An unlocked phone exposed a private chat-channel QR image. The supplied `raw_packets.txt` contains captured radio frames. Determine the radio technology, channel key, Catherine's location, what she plans to bring back, the closest likely specialty shop, and the agency that might disrupt the trip.

The QR image and packet text are available in `IMAGES/` in this repository. The solving guide omits the decoded key and conversation.
