# Vessel Tracking

A recovered vessel-tracking CSV is supplied with the challenge. Use it to investigate a suspected illegal fishing incident. No paid software or website account is required.

1. Which RFMO Convention Area was the vessel operating in during this period? An abbreviation or full name is accepted.
2. What was the vessel's MMSI?
3. Under what flag state was the vessel registered at the time of the incident?
4. What was the full name of the company under which the vessel operated for its longest known continuous period before 2025?

The tracking CSV is challenge evidence and is not stored in this repository.
