# Vessel Tracking: how to solve

## Steps

1. Load the CSV and inspect its time range, coordinate bounds, speeds, and gaps. Plot the positions in time order to see the route and identify slow movement or encounters.
2. Compare the positions with official RFMO convention-area maps. A vessel's location is more reliable for the area question than its name or flag.
3. Search incident reports using the **date, time, and coordinates together**. Several vessels can be active in the same broad region on the same dates. Match the track to a documented encounter before accepting an identity.
4. Use that report to confirm the vessel identifier and the flag **at the incident date**. Historical flag changes can make a current vessel page misleading.
5. For the operating-company question, compare dated *operator* intervals and calculate each uninterrupted duration. Keep owner and operator histories distinct, and do not join separate intervals across a gap.
