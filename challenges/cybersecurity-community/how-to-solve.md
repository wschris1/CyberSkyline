# Cybersecurity Community: how to solve

## Research approach

Use the organizers' own histories and archives where possible. Separate an event's founding year from the year a competition or village began within that event. For attendance claims, use figures available by 2025 and compare conferences on the same basis.

## Steps

1. Open the [DEF CON archive](https://defcon.org/html/links/dc-archives.html) and find the earliest conference listing. Record its calendar year.
2. Look for the first Pwn2Own event in the competition organizer's history. Note the host conference, rather than the competition organizer.
3. Compare 2025 attendance figures in the major conferences' official recaps. Identify which describes itself as the largest information security conference by attendee count.
4. Read the [Hardware Hacking Village's history announcement](https://github.com/DCHHV/dchhv.github.io/blob/master/_posts/2018-06-30-special-announcement.md). It names the DEF CON edition where the village was established. Match that edition to the year in the DEF CON archive.
5. Consult the NSA's Centers of Academic Excellence program history. Expand the co-sponsor's acronym to its full federal agency name.
6. In the same CAE history, distinguish the program's starting year from later changes in sponsorship or program name.

Check the dates and names against more than one source before submitting. Keep the final responses in the CTF platform, not in this repository.
