# Water-Damaged Barcode

Someone taped a water-damaged barcode sticker to a friend's laptop case with no further context. Decode the barcode shown in [the attached image](question.png).

The visible printed characters are `345190028411`; the remaining characters are damaged or obscured.

1. What is the last digit of the barcode? (20 points)
2. Which institution likely issued the barcode? Include the abbreviated U.S. state, using the format `InstitutionName, NY`. (55 points)
