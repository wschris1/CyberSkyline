# Cyber Command

A small subset of Liber8tion is believed to belong to a society of birdwatchers and may be using its forum to transmit sensitive information. A tap on a misconfigured server exposed backend authentication traffic. Can you crack the users' passwords?

The passwords are believed to contain **at least 10 characters**, **exactly one uppercase character**, and **exactly one special character**.

The supplied artifacts are `traffic.pcap` and `sslkeylog.txt`. Obtain them separately; neither file is stored in this repository.
